Passive TDM clock expectations (measure, do not assume)
=======================================================

FSC/FS   ~8 kHz
DCL/BCLK ~1.024 MHz for 8 x 16-bit slots
MCL      ~4.096 MHz if present

These are nominal values from the recovered 8-slot geometry.  Actual
frequency, duty cycle, polarity and driver direction must be measured with a
scope/logic analyzer.  These files are not evidence that SC14448 is running.
